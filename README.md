## Project Overview

This project is an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard built in Microsoft Excel using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPI Cards, and Slicers. The dashboard provides insights into employee attrition, workforce demographics, and salary trends, helping users analyze HR data efficiently.

---

## Objectives

- Analyze employee attrition across departments and job roles.
- Monitor key HR metrics using KPI cards.
- Build an interactive dashboard with dynamic filtering.
- Present HR data through meaningful visualizations.

---

## Dashboard Features

### KPI Cards
- Total Employees
- Employees Left
- Average Age
- Average Monthly Income
- Average Years at Company

### Interactive Charts
- Employee Attrition by Department
- Employee Attrition by Job Role
- Employee Attrition by Gender
- Employee Attrition by Business Travel
- Employee Attrition by OverTime
- Average Monthly Income by Department

### Interactive Filters
- Job Role
- Gender
- Department

---

## Key Insights

- Research & Development has the highest employee attrition.
- Laboratory Technicians have the highest attrition among job roles.
- Employees working overtime experience higher attrition than those who do not.
- Employee attrition varies across business travel categories.
- Average monthly income differs between departments.
